Long description (150 words)

eCon Growth is the software company behind Command HVAC, the AI Operating System HVAC companies run on. Co-founded in 2025 by Kristopher Cravens (Founder & CEO, ten years inside HVAC operations) and Watson Wheeler (Co-Founder & COO, operations and exit-strategy focus), the company builds AI products for HVAC contractors running 2–50+ trucks — fixing the structural problem that keeps most HVAC businesses stuck under $5M in revenue: dispatch that lives in one person's head, leads that fall through after hours, and an owner who can't step out of operations.

Its flagship products — Command HVAC (the AI Operating System) and Roger (the 24/7 AI voice agent that answers and books every call in under three seconds) — are powered by Anthropic's Claude. Together they replace owner-dependent operations with systematic AI dispatch, real-time visibility, coaching analytics, and weekly business intelligence.
