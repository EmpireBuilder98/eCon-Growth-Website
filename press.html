Boilerplate — long

eCon Growth is an AI consultancy for serious operators: the owners running real businesses who know AI could take work off their plate but can't see where it fits. Rather than selling tools or training, eCon Growth identifies the single highest-leverage system in a business, builds it end to end, and hands back an operation that no longer depends on its owner's attention to keep moving. Work spans operations, marketing, and financial planning, with deployed systems including Command HVAC, an AI operating system for HVAC owners, and Roger, an AI voice agent.
