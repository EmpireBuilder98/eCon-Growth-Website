Command HVAC vs FieldEdge.

An honest comparison of Command HVAC by eCon Growth and FieldEdge for HVAC contractors. Both are real tools that work for real operations — they're just designed for different stages and philosophies. Here's how they actually compare.

TL;DR
FieldEdge is a long-established HVAC field-service platform with strong QuickBooks integration. Command HVAC is the AI-native alternative built for HVAC contractors who want an operating system, not a database.

Where each one wins

FieldEdge is the right call when…

  • HVAC-focused with deep QuickBooks integration and accounting-friendly workflows
  • Established product with a long track record in the HVAC industry
  • Strong inventory and parts management features
  • Familiar to HVAC owners who have used it for years

Command HVAC is the right call when…

  • AI-native architecture — voice agent, dispatch decisions, and intelligence all powered by Anthropic's Claude
  • Modern interface designed for the way HVAC operations run today, not 2010
  • Roger answers every call 24/7; FieldEdge needs a human on the phone
  • Real-time visibility built for the owner without manual reporting setup
  • Onboarding measured in days, not months
  • Co-founder Kristopher Cravens spent 10 years inside HVAC operations — built Command HVAC from the field perspective, not from accounting

The honest take

FieldEdge is a real product that has earned its market position. We're not going to pretend otherwise. But Command HVAC is built on a different philosophy: an HVAC business is not a database with a calendar bolted on. It is an operation that runs on dispatch, voice intake, technician judgment, and the owner's ability to see what's happening. An AI Operating System fits that operation. A general-purpose field-service tool, no matter how polished, does not.

If you've outgrown the calendar and you're tired of being the bottleneck on every dispatch decision, Command HVAC is the system designed for that exact moment. If your business runs more like an enterprise IT department, FieldEdge may serve you better.
