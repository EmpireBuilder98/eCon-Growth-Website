# eCon Growth — SEO Distribution & Backlink Kit

This file is your playbook for getting eCon Growth cited everywhere that compounds search and AI visibility. Each entry below has copy you can paste, a target URL, and the expected backlink/citation impact.

**Why this matters more than schema:** Backlinks and authoritative citations are still the #1 ranking factor on Google and the strongest signal AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) use to decide whose answer to surface. Schema and structured data are necessary but not sufficient — backlinks are the rocket fuel.

---

## Tier 1 — Submit this week (highest ROI, ~2 hours total)

### 1. Capterra (HVAC Software directory)
- **Submit at:** https://about.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up/
- **Category:** HVAC Software / Field Service Management Software
- **Why:** Capterra is one of the top three SaaS directories Google trusts. AI engines pull from Capterra reviews when answering "best HVAC software."
- **Effort:** 30 min initial submission, then ~30 days for approval.

### 2. G2 (B2B software reviews)
- **Submit at:** https://www.g2.com/products/new
- **Category:** Field Service Management / Business Operations / AI Voice Agent
- **Why:** G2 is the most-cited SaaS source by AI engines. A G2 listing with even five reviews creates a powerful citation node.
- **Effort:** 20 min listing, then incentivize 5 customers to leave reviews.

### 3. GetApp (Capterra subsidiary, separate listing)
- **Submit at:** https://vendors.getapp.com/
- **Category:** HVAC Software
- **Effort:** 15 min — uses same data as Capterra so it's quick once Capterra is done.

### 4. Software Advice (HVAC Software category specifically)
- **Submit at:** https://www.softwareadvice.com/vendors/
- **Category:** HVAC Software
- **Why:** Buyers specifically searching "HVAC software" land on Software Advice's category page. Being listed there = inbound qualified leads.

### 5. Product Hunt
- **Submit at:** https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new
- **Why:** A successful Product Hunt launch (top 5 of the day) drives 500+ backlinks within 48 hours from blogs and aggregators that scrape Product Hunt. Plus, Producthunt itself is heavily indexed by AI engines.
- **Best timing:** Tuesday or Wednesday at 12:01 AM PST. Have a pre-built supporter list ready.

### 6. Crunchbase
- **Submit at:** https://about.crunchbase.com/add-company/
- **Why:** Crunchbase is in nearly every AI engine's training/citation set. It's the canonical company-info source AI uses for "who founded eCon Growth?" type queries.
- **Effort:** 20 min for company profile + founder profile.

## Tier 3 — Domain authority builders (medium effort, high compounding)

### 11. LinkedIn company posts (you, weekly)
- **Frequency:** 2x/week minimum.
- **Format:** "Lesson from inside HVAC operations" + a link to the relevant blog post on econ-growth.com.
- **Why:** LinkedIn is the highest-DA domain that lets you publish freely. Each post that links back to econ-growth.com is a real backlink in Google's eyes.

### 12. Reddit (specific subreddits, sparingly)
- **Targets:** r/HVAC, r/sweatystartup, r/Entrepreneur (HVAC operator persona), r/ChatGPT (AI angle), r/ArtificialIntelligence
- **Approach:** Answer questions genuinely with eCon Growth as a brief mention, not a pitch. Reddit is heavily cited by AI engines because models trust forum-vetted answers.

### 13. Quora answers
- **Search and answer:** "best HVAC software," "AI for HVAC business," "how to scale an HVAC business," "what is an AI voice agent"
- **Why:** Quora content is foundational training data for several AI engines.

### 14. Indie Hackers
- **Submit at:** https://www.indiehackers.com/products/new
- **Why:** Strong domain authority, AI-engine-friendly community.

### 15. HVAC industry publications (highest signal)
- **Targets:** ACHR News, Contracting Business, HVACR Business, HVAC Insider
- **Approach:** Pitch a guest article based on one of the existing blog posts. The byline backlink is gold.

## Tracking

After submitting, log each on a spreadsheet with:
- Date submitted
- Status (pending / live / rejected)
- Backlink URL once live
- Domain Rating (use ahrefs.com/website-authority-checker or similar)
- Whether it has been cited in an AI engine answer (test in ChatGPT / Claude / Perplexity)

Re-test "what is eCon Growth?" and "best AI software for HVAC contractors?" in each AI engine monthly. Track which directories show up as citations.
