## ⚠️ Common launch mistakes to avoid

- ❌ Launching without supporter-list pre-mobilization — you'll get ~5 upvotes from organic traffic, that's it
- ❌ Begging for upvotes ("upvote me!"). PH community downvotes this.
- ❌ Going silent in the comments. Respond to every comment for the first 6 hours.
- ❌ Launching on Monday/Friday — half the traffic of Tue/Wed
- ❌ Skipping the gallery images. Underperforms by 40%.
- ❌ Generic tagline ("AI software for businesses"). Be specific about WHO and WHAT.
- ❌ First comment is a feature list. Make it personal. Tell the founder story.
