## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't post the launch URL in group chats / Slack / Discord without context. Looks spammy.
- ❌ Don't ask people to "upvote 50 of my posts" or vague "support me!" — be specific.
- ❌ Don't send the same message to your whole email list. Personalize the first line.
- ❌ Don't beg in your PH comments. Engage with substance instead.
- ❌ Don't @ tag random PH influencers asking for upvotes. Most ignore, some report you.
